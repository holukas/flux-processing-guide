---
title: "L3.1 · Storage correction"
---

::: {.callout-note title="Summary"}
L3.1 adds the storage term to the fluxes, and the CO2 flux becomes NEE. The storage term comes from profile measurements or, without them, from the one-point storage term of EddyPro.
:::

- **NEE:** CO2 flux + CO2 storage term. The name changes from `FC` to `NEE`.
- **Other fluxes** (LE, H, N2O, CH4): the flux + its storage term. They keep their name, so the suffix `_L3.1` marks them as storage-corrected, e.g. `LE_L3.1`.
- **Storage term:** ideally from profile measurements. Without them, the one-point storage term that EddyPro calculates in the L1 run.
- **Missing storage terms:** without a storage term, the flux record is lost. Gap-filling the storage term keeps these records.
- **No flags** are used in this step.
- **Grasslands:** the storage term is normally negligible. It matters for forests.
