The final [fluxrun](scripts/fluxrun.md) run. All later steps use its results.

- **Boxes:** 6 and 7 in the [processing chain](Processing_Chain.md).
- **Input:** the same raw data files as the [L0 preliminary run](L0.md).
- **Meteo input:** 6 screened meteo variables, from [meteo for EddyPro](Meteo_For_EddyPro.md). See [Biomet data](data/Biomet_Data.md).
- **Time lags:** window size, nominal lags and constant lags from the L0 run.
- **Output:** the EddyPro fluxnet output file, 30 min, with `TIMESTAMP_END`.
- **Runs on:** your own computer.
- **Next step:** [flux processing chain](Flux_Processing_Chain.md).

## Differences to L0

The L1 run does not depend on the L0 run, but uses what L0 showed. Compared to L0, the L1 run often has:

- a narrower time lag window, e.g. 10 s instead of 20 s.
- a different spectral correction, e.g. Fratini et al. (2012) instead of Moncrieff et al. (1997) for a closed-path IRGA.
- the 6 meteo variables as additional input.
- additional output, e.g. the random uncertainty.
- the setup given as exactly as possible, e.g. the separation between the sonic anemometer and the gas analyzer.

## Time lags

- **IRGA (CO2, H2O):** covariance maximization in a narrow window around the nominal lag. Without a clear covariance peak in the window, EddyPro uses the nominal lag.
- **QCL and LGR (N2O, CH4):** a constant lag, per year or per period in which the lag changed.

## Open-path IRGA

- **Closed-path IRGA** (e.g. LI-7200): the fluxes do not change after L1.
- **Open-path IRGA** (e.g. LI-7500): CO2 fluxes may need a correction for the self-heating of the sensor, after L1. It is not part of the chart.
- **Off-season uptake correction in EddyPro:** developed for vertically mounted instruments. It does not account for an inclined IRGA. In a comparison with parallel closed-path fluxes, it performed poorly.
- **When it is needed:** the correction was developed for cold conditions, mainly below -10 °C.
- **Without parallel measurements:** applying the correction is risky, because its effect cannot be checked. With a closed-path IRGA next to the open-path one, the correction can be scaled to match it (Kittler et al., 2017).

## Other settings

- **Density fluctuations (WPL):** needed for open-path IRGAs. Not needed for closed-path analyzers that record dry mixing ratios.
- **Spectral assessment file:** one per year, or per period with its own setup. A very short period can use the file of a neighbouring period.
- **(Co)spectra:** the spectral correction after Fratini et al. (2012) needs them. Without (co)spectra, EddyPro uses Moncrieff et al. (1997) instead.
