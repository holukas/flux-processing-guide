Flux Processing Guide

Modified

29 September 2026

NoteSummary

This guide describes the chain from raw eddy covariance and meteo data to quality-controlled fluxes and the flux product. The chart shows each step as a numbered box that links to its page.

Numbers in brackets refer to the boxes in the chart.

Time resolution

The pills at the bottom of the boxes show the time resolution of the data.

  • Eddy covariance raw data: 20 Hz. The fluxes calculated from them are 30 min, from L0 on.
  • Meteo data: high resolution in the logger files, the upload and the raw bucket: mostly 1 min, sometimes 10 s or 30 min.
  • From the meteo screening on: 30 min. The screening resamples the data.

Eddy covariance

  • Raw data logging (1, 2): a logging script on the data logger at the site records the raw data at 20 Hz.
    • sonicread writes binary files (1). Format: EC raw data: sonicread.
    • rECord writes CSV files in TOA5 format (2). Format: EC raw data: rECord.
  • Raw data conversion (3, 16): bico converts the binary files from sonicread to CSV files.
    • Files from rECord are already CSV files and skip this step.
  • L0 · Preliminary run (4, 5): fluxrun runs EddyPro on the raw CSV files, with relaxed settings and a wide time lag window.
    • The preliminary fluxes are used for checks: complete data, plausible fluxes, time lags, wind direction.
    • They are used to refine the settings for L1, e.g. the time lag.
  • L1 · Final flux run (6, 7): the final fluxrun run, with the refined settings, on the same raw data.
    • It also uses 6 meteo variables as input (17).
    • The result is the EddyPro output, 30 min. All later steps use it.

Meteo

  • Meteo logging (8): meteo stations on site record the meteo data.
  • Meteo upload (9, 10): dataflow uploads the logger files to the raw bucket of the InfluxDB database.
  • Meteo screening (11, 12): a diive notebook screens the meteo data, resamples them to 30 min and uploads them to the processed bucket.
  • Meteo for EddyPro (17): a diive notebook formats 6 screened meteo variables for the L1 run.
  • Additional meteo for dataset (18): diive notebooks merge the screened meteo data from both screening tools, correct them on the 30-min data and write them for the dataset.
    • They are drivers for the diive flux post-processing chain.
    • They also go into the flux product.

Management

  • Management data (15): field records.
    • Used at L3.2 (outlier flagging) and L4.1 (gap-filling).
    • Go into the flux product, if relevant.

Flux processing

  • Flux post-processing chain (13): the notebook FluxPostProcessingChain.ipynb processes the L1 fluxes from L2 to L4.2.
    • Meteo drivers are used at L3.3 (USTAR threshold), L4.1 (gap-filling) and L4.2 (partitioning).
  • Flux product (14): fluxes, meteo data and management data.

Scripts

  • sonicread logs eddy covariance raw data on site as binary files.
  • rECord logs eddy covariance raw data on site as CSV files.
  • bico converts the binary raw data written by sonicread to CSV files for EddyPro.
  • fluxrun runs EddyPro on the raw data files, converted by bico or written directly by rECord.
  • dataflow finds logger files, assigns a filetype and uploads the data to the database.
  • diive screens the meteo data and runs the flux post-processing chain.
