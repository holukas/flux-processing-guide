---
title: "L4.2 · Partitioning"
---

Partitions NEE and ET into their components, using the meteo drivers.

- **NEE:** partitioned into GPP and RECO.
- **Methods:** the daytime method (Lasslop et al., 2010) is generally preferred. Also run the nighttime method (Reichstein et al., 2005) and compare the results.
- **Input:** the gap-filled NEE from [L4.1](L4.1.md). FLUXNET partitions MDS gap-filled NEE instead.
- **Further reading:** Wutzler et al. (2018).
