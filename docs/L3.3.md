title: "L3.3 · USTAR threshold detection"
---

::: {.callout-note title="Summary"}
L3.3 detects the USTAR threshold on the highest-quality nighttime NEE and flags records with lower USTAR. Three thresholds, `CUT_16`, `CUT_50` and `CUT_84`, give three versions of NEE, N2O and CH4. H, LE and ET get no threshold.
:::

- **Flags only:** records with low turbulence get a flag. No data are removed in this step.
- **Threshold:** constant across all years, or seasonal. It can also come from a FLUXNET threshold detection for the site (Pastorello et al., 2020).
- **Highest-quality data:** the detection needs the best nighttime NEE. The flags from L2 and L3.2 are combined into a [QCF](QCF.md), and only NEE with QCF `0` is used.
- **Applying the threshold:** NEE below the threshold is flagged. The remaining NEE can still contain records with QCF `1`, depending on whether records with QCF `1` are kept.
- **N2O and CH4:** the NEE threshold is also applied to them, but this is debated.
- **H, LE and ET:** no threshold. It has not been shown that CO2 advection also affects the energy fluxes (Pastorello et al., 2020).

## Three USTAR scenarios

Following Pastorello et al. (2020), three thresholds give three versions of NEE, N2O and CH4:

| Scenario | Threshold |
|---|---|
| `CUT_50` | 50th percentile of the detected thresholds, the best estimate |
| `CUT_16` | 16th percentile, lower threshold |
| `CUT_84` | 84th percentile, higher threshold |

`CUT` stands for constant USTAR threshold.
