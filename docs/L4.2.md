title: "L4.2 · Partitioning"
---

::: {.callout-note title="Summary"}
L4.2 partitions NEE into GPP and RECO, using the gap-filled NEE from [L4.1](L4.1.md) and the meteo drivers. The daytime method (Lasslop et al., 2010) is generally preferred, with the nighttime method (Reichstein et al., 2005) for comparison.
:::

- **NEE:** partitioned into GPP and RECO.
- **ET:** partitioned into its components.
- **Methods:** the daytime method (Lasslop et al., 2010) is generally preferred. The nighttime method (Reichstein et al., 2005) is also run, and the results are compared.
- **Input:** the gap-filled NEE from [L4.1](L4.1.md). FLUXNET partitions MDS gap-filled NEE instead.
- **Further reading:** Wutzler et al. (2018).
