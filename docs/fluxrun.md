---
title: "fluxrun"
---

Runs EddyPro on the raw data files and manages its settings and output folders. It runs twice:

1. A **Level-0 run** gives preliminary fluxes.
2. The Level-0 fluxes are used to refine the settings, e.g. the time lag, for the final **Level-1 run**. The Level-1 run also uses the screened meteo data.
