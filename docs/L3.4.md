---
title: "L3.4 · Overall quality flag QCF"
---

Combines all flags into the overall quality flag QCF.

- **Input:** the test flags from [L2](L2.md), [L3.2](L3.2.md) and [L3.3](L3.3.md).
- **One QCF per flux**, and per USTAR scenario.
- **Same logic** as the QCF of the [meteo screening](Meteo_Screening.md). Only the tests differ.

## Values

| QCF | Quality | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `0` | best: all tests passed | use |
| `1` | OK: no test gave `2`, one or two tests gave `1` | can be used for budgets; at most sites, reject nighttime NEE with QCF `1` |
| `2` | bad: at least one test gave `2`, or three or more gave `1` | always reject |

## Calculation

The test flags are added up:

| Sum of the flags | QCF |
|---|---|
| `0` | `0` |
| `1` | `1` |
| `2`, from two flags of `1` | `1` |
| `2`, from one flag of `2` | `2` |
| more than `2` | `2` |

- **Hard flags:** tests that give only `0` or `2`. One failed hard test makes the QCF `2`.
- **Soft flags:** tests that can give `1`. One or two of them still give QCF `1`.

## After L3.4

- **Filtered flux:** the flux with QCF `2` removed, and nighttime NEE with QCF `1` removed. The gap-filling in [L4.1](L4.1.md) uses it.
- **Highest-quality flux:** only records with QCF `0`.
